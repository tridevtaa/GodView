Godview

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Godview (godview.in, and the Godview app on Google Play) is a school management app for schools in India. School owners, admins, principals and teachers use it to keep student records, attendance, fees, results and transport; parents use it to follow their child. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have.

Schools decide, we look after it

Each school decides what to record about its students and staff, and is responsible for it. Godview stores and processes that data on the school’s behalf, only to run the service for that school. Each school’s data is kept apart from every other school’s, and people only see what their role allows: for example, a teacher sees their own classes, and a parent sees only their own child.

What we collect

  • Staff accounts: name, email address, phone number, role, and the profile details the school adds (such as designation, joining date and documents).
  • Students (added by the school): name, class and section, date of birth, gender, admission details, photo, parents’ names and phone numbers, address, and optionally a home location pin for school transport; attendance, homework, exam results, fees and payments; and documents the school uploads (such as a birth certificate, transfer certificate or a copy of an Aadhaar card). Aadhaar numbers are stored only as their last 4 digits.
  • Parents: phone number, name, and the messages and requests they send to the school.
  • Use of the app: notification settings for your device, basic technical logs, and how screens are used (see Microsoft Clarity below).
  • The assistant (Premium schools, owners, admins and principals): the questions you type or speak and its replies. Speech is turned into text by your browser or phone before it reaches us.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use it for anything other than running Godview for your school.

Who else handles data

We use these services to run Godview. Each handles data only to provide its part of the service.

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in. Data is stored in India (Mumbai) and encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the website and app.
  • Microsoft Clarity: anonymous heatmaps of how screens are used, to improve the app. Inside the staff and parent apps, the content of the screen is masked, so student and personal details are not recorded.
  • OpenStreetMap: map images and address search on the transport map.
  • AI services (Premium features): AI insights and the assistant send the relevant school figures (which can include student names) and your question to Groq or Anthropic to write an answer, and the assistant’s replies to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to be spoken. These services run outside India and are used with settings that do not keep or train on the data.
  • Push notifications: delivered through your browser’s or phone’s push service (for example Google on Android).

Children

Children do not sign in to Godview. Information about children is added by their school, and parents see their own child’s information through the parent app. To see, correct or remove a child’s information, contact the school; the school can do it in Godview, and we will help.

How long we keep data

We keep a school’s data while the school uses Godview. When a school stops using Godview or asks us to delete its data, we delete it, except where the law requires us to keep records (such as fee payments) for longer. Deleted data disappears from backups as they are replaced.

Your choices and rights

  • See, correct or delete your information: ask your school, or write to us at the address below.
  • Delete your Godview account: see Delete your account.
  • Turn notifications off at any time from the account menu or your phone’s settings.

These match your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including raising a grievance with us and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited by role and by school, and only the last 4 digits of Aadhaar numbers are kept. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell your school and the authorities as the law requires.

Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints: support@godview.in. We reply within 7 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
