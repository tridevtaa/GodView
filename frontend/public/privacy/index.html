Godview

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Godview is a school management app for schools in India. School owners, admins, principals and teachers use it to keep student records, admissions, attendance, homework, results, fees, certificates and school transport. Parents use it to follow their child and talk to the school.

This policy covers the website godview.in, the Godview app on Google Play (in.godview.app) and the Godview app on other app stores (together, “Godview”, “we”, “us”). It explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. If you do not agree with it, please do not use Godview.

1. Schools decide, we look after it

Each school decides what to record about its students, applicants, parents and staff, and is responsible for that data. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the school is the Data Fiduciary and Godview is its Data Processor: we store and process the data only on the school’s behalf and only to run Godview for that school.

Each school’s data is kept apart from every other school’s. Inside a school, people see only what their role allows. For example, a teacher sees their own classes, fee details are limited to the owner and admins, and a parent sees only their own children.

Godview itself decides how to use a small amount of data about how the service is used (section 2.8) and from people who contact us (section 2.9). For that data, we are the Data Fiduciary.

2. What we collect

2.1 Staff accounts and profiles

  • Name, email address, phone number, photo, role, designation, date of birth, joining date and who they report to.
  • Classes and subjects taught, timetable, and the work they record in the app.
  • Staff KYC, where the school asks for it: the school policy the staff member agreed to, and copies of identity and address proof documents (such as Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, passport or voter ID).

2.2 Students

Added by the school:

  • Name, photo, class and section, roll and admission numbers, date of birth, gender, category, religion, admission details, parents’ names and phone numbers, email address and home address.
  • Transport details: bus route, bus stop, and optionally a home location pin placed on a map by the school.
  • Attendance, homework, diary entries, exam marks and results, and certificates issued (such as transfer or bona fide certificates).
  • Fee dues, concessions and payments.
  • Documents the school uploads, such as a birth certificate, transfer certificate or a copy of an Aadhaar card. Aadhaar numbers are never kept as a separate field, only inside a document the school chooses to upload.

2.3 Admission enquiries and applicants

When a family enquires about or registers for admission, the school records the applicant’s name, date of birth, class applied for, parents’ names, phone numbers and email address, address, documents (such as a birth certificate or Aadhaar copy) and any registration fee paid.

2.4 Parents

  • Phone number and name, which the school links to their children.
  • The messages, leave requests, photos and other requests they send to the school, and which messages they have read.

2.5 Fee payments

For every fee payment recorded, Godview keeps the amount, method (such as cash, UPI or cheque), reference number, receipt number and date. To protect schools against mistakes and fraud, the payment log also keeps who recorded or cancelled each payment, and the IP address, browser and device they used. Godview does not take payments itself and never sees card numbers or bank login details.

2.6 School content

Notices, calendar events, datesheets, timetables, syllabus, homework and the photos, videos and files attached to them, and the school’s own profile (name, logo, address, contact details and location).

2.7 The assistant and AI insights

On Premium plans, owners, admins and principals can ask the Godview assistant questions and see AI insights about their school. We keep the questions typed or spoken and the replies, and count how many are asked, to run the feature and apply fair use limits. Speech is turned into text by your phone or browser before it reaches us; we do not receive or store voice recordings.

2.8 Devices and use of the app

  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a push token for your device, so we can send you messages, notices, reminders and fee alerts.
  • Basic technical logs (such as time, IP address and errors) kept by our hosting providers to run and secure the service.
  • How screens are used, through Microsoft Clarity (section 5). Inside the staff and parent apps, all screen content is masked, so student and personal details are never recorded.

2.9 People who contact us

If you ask for a call back on godview.in, we keep your name, school name and phone number to get in touch about Godview. If you email us, we keep your email and what you send.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to tell you about new messages, notices and reminders. Optional; you can turn them off at any time.
  • Photos, camera and files: only when you choose to add a photo or document. Godview reads only the files you pick.
  • Microphone: only when you tap to speak to the assistant. Speech is turned into text on your device. Optional.

Godview does not ask for your location, contacts, call logs or SMS.

4. How we use data

  • To provide Godview to the school: keep records, show each person what their role allows, send notifications and produce reports.
  • To sign people in, keep accounts and data secure, and prevent and look into misuse or fraud.
  • To answer questions, fix problems and give support.
  • To understand how screens are used, so we can improve Godview.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, show ads, build advertising profiles or use school data to train AI models. We do not use data for any purpose the school has not asked for.

5. Who else handles data

We use these service providers to run Godview. Each handles data only to provide its part of the service, under its own security and privacy terms, and may not use it for its own purposes.

  • Supabase: database, file storage, sign-in and server functions. School data is stored in India (Mumbai) and encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the website and app securely.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and browser push services: deliver notifications to your device.
  • Microsoft Clarity: heatmaps of how screens are used, with screen content masked inside the staff and parent apps.
  • Google Maps Platform and OpenStreetMap: maps, address search and directions on the transport and location screens. They receive the map area shown and any address you search for.
  • Groq and Anthropic (Premium features): AI insights and the assistant send your question and the relevant school figures, which can include student names, to write an answer. Both are used with settings that do not keep the data or train on it.
  • Google Cloud Text-to-Speech (Premium features): turns the assistant’s replies into speech. It receives only the reply text.

Apart from these providers, we share personal data only when the school asks us to, when the law requires it (for example a valid order from a court or government authority), to protect people’s safety or the security of Godview, or as part of a merger or sale of Godview, in which case this policy continues to apply to the data.

6. Where data is stored

School data is stored in India. Some providers in section 5 (such as the AI, notification and analytics services) may process data outside India, only to provide their service and as permitted by Indian law.

7. Children

Godview is used by schools and parents, not by children. Children do not sign in to Godview. Information about children is added by their school, which is responsible for getting any consent needed from parents under the DPDP Act. Parents see their own child’s information through the parent app.

We do not track children, build profiles of them, or show them or their parents any advertising. To see, correct or remove a child’s information, contact the school; the school can do it in Godview, and we will help.

8. How long we keep data

  • School data is kept while the school uses Godview. When a school stops using Godview or asks us to delete its data, we delete it within 90 days, except records the law requires us or the school to keep for longer (such as fee payment records).
  • When a school removes a student, applicant, parent or staff member, their details are deleted or kept only as the school’s records require.
  • When you delete your account, your sign-in and personal profile are deleted within 30 days.
  • Push tokens are deleted when you turn notifications off, sign out, or the device stops accepting notifications.
  • Call back requests are deleted within 12 months if the school does not become a customer.
  • Deleted data leaves our backups as they are replaced, within 30 days.

9. Your choices and rights

Under the DPDP Act and other Indian law, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data held about you and who it has been shared with.
  • Ask for your data to be corrected, completed, updated or erased.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, for example by turning off notifications.
  • Name someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot do so yourself.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Because the school controls its records, requests about student, applicant, parent or staff records are best made to the school, which can act on them in Godview. You can also write to us, and we will pass your request on and help the school act on it.

Delete your account: use Delete account in the app’s account menu, or see Delete your account, which works without the app installed.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited by school and by role through rules enforced in the database itself, not only in the app. Documents are kept in private storage that only permitted people can open, and fee payments keep a log of every change. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects personal data, we will tell the school, the people affected and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about privacy, write to our Grievance Officer at support@godview.in. We acknowledge every request within 7 days and resolve it within 30 days.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in an important way, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
