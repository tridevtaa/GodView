Delete your account

You can delete your Godview account at any time, whether you are school staff or a parent.

In the app

  1. Open the menu at the top right and tap your name to open My profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

If you are the only owner of a school, the app can’t delete your account, because the school would have nobody to run it. Email us and we’ll help you hand it over or close the school.

Without the app

Email support@godview.in from the email address you sign in with, or tell us the phone number you sign in with, with your name and your school’s name.

Email us to delete my account

We confirm we have your request within 7 days and delete the account within 30 days. If we can’t tell the request is really from you, we’ll ask you to confirm from your phone or email first.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email or phone and password).
  • Your photo and the personal details you added, such as ID, emergency contact, email and address.
  • Staff: your access to the school, your classes, and the documents you uploaded when you joined.
  • Notifications on your devices, and your use of the assistant.

What stays with the school

Records that belong to your school are part of the school’s own records and are not removed when your account is deleted: for example, your name on the school’s staff or parent list, a child’s attendance, results and fee payments, notices, and messages you sent to the school. To have a child’s records removed, ask the school; the school can delete them in Godview. Fee payment records may be kept for as long as the law requires.

Staff: your school’s owner can also remove your access at any time from Staff in the app.
