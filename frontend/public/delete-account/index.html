Godview

Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Godview account at any time, whether you are school staff or a parent. You don’t need the app installed to do this.

How to ask

  1. Send an email from the email address you sign in with, or mention the phone number you sign in with, to support@godview.in.
  2. Include your name and your school’s name.

Email us to delete my account

We confirm we have your request within 7 days, check with your school where needed, and delete the account within 30 days. If we can’t tell the request is really from you, we’ll ask you to confirm from your phone or email first.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email or phone and password) and your profile details.
  • Your notification settings and devices.
  • Your use of the assistant.

What stays with the school

Records that belong to your school are part of the school’s own records and are not removed when your account is deleted: for example, a child’s attendance, results and fee payments, notices, and messages you sent to the school. To have a child’s records removed, ask the school; the school can delete them in Godview. Fee payment records may be kept for as long as the law requires.

Staff: your school’s owner can also remove your access at any time from Staff in the app.

See also our privacy policy.
